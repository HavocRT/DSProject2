# DSProject2
Foundations of Data Science project 2

# title
Advanced Diabetes Prediction Model

# description
The project aims to create a model that can predict diabetes at an advanced model. Not just by looking at the demographic details of the patient's records but also by predicting the possibility of the person having heart disease and hypertension.

Heart disease and hypertension are two big causes for diabetes and they usually precede diabetes in most patients. Our model aims to predict the chance of a person having diabetes in the near future by considering the main symptoms and factors that go into a diabetes diagnosis, while also considering factors that go into hypertension and heart disease.
