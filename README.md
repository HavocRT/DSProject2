# DSProject2
Foundations of Data Science project 2

# title
Advanced Heart Disease Prediction Model

# description
The project aims to create a model that can predict heart disease at an advanced model. Not just by looking at the demographic details of the patient's records but also by predicting the possibility of the person having diabetes and hypertension.

Diabetes and hypertension are two big causes for heart disease and they usually precede heart disease in most patients. Our model aims to predict the chance of a person having heart disease in the future by considering the main symptoms and factors that go into a heart disease diagnosis, while also considering factors that go into hypertension and diabetes.
